## Disclaimer

**IKEA Gifting** is the project name used for this educational analytics case study. This project should not be interpreted as an official IKEA business analysis or as an endorsement by IKEA. The analysis and visualizations are intended for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes. Any business interpretations are based on the dataset and project assumptions.

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on customer analytics for a gifting-focused retail business scenario. The objective is to understand customer behavior, explore purchasing patterns, and translate transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The project follows a business-oriented analytics approach, starting with the identification of business requirements and moving towards data analysis and dashboard-based reporting.

Rather than simply presenting raw numbers, the goal is to understand what the data reveals about customers and how those insights can support better business decisions.

The project combines business requirement documentation, customer data analysis, and data visualization to create a structured view of the business.

---

## My Thinking Process

I approached this project from a business perspective rather than starting directly with dashboard development.

Before analyzing the dataset, I focused on understanding the business requirements and identifying the kind of insights that would be valuable to decision-makers.

The Business Requirement Document (BRD) helped establish the project scope and provided direction for the analysis.

The intention was to move beyond basic reporting and use customer data to answer meaningful business questions, identify patterns, and present insights in a way that business stakeholders can understand.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw data alone does not provide a clear understanding of customer behavior or business performance.

The business needs a structured way to analyze customer information, understand purchasing patterns, identify potential gifting opportunities, and make informed decisions using data.

Without a consolidated analytical view, it becomes difficult to identify meaningful trends and convert customer data into actionable business insights.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze customer data to understand purchasing behavior.
- Identify meaningful patterns and trends in customer transactions.
- Explore customer preferences and gifting-related opportunities.
- Convert raw data into useful business insights.
- Present analytical findings through a clear and structured dashboard.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.

## Business Questions

The project is designed around the following analytical areas:

- What insights can be identified from the available customer and transaction data?
- What patterns and trends can be observed in customer purchasing behavior?
- Which aspects of customer activity can help identify gifting opportunities?
- How can customer and business performance be monitored effectively?
- How can the findings be translated into practical business recommendations?
